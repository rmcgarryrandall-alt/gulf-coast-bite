The local pattern

Around Pensacola, the East Bay drains and bayou mouths fish all season, and the pass pilings load up during the run. The seawalls and bridge rubble give shore anglers a legitimate shot on any falling tide.

Flounder stack where falling water drops bait over an edge: drains, cuts, channel edges, and the down-current side of pilings. October's run pours fish toward the Gulf, and both states protect the tail of it, so plan harvest trips for September into mid-October.

Common questions

When is the flounder run at Pensacola?

October is the classic run month as fish leave the bays for the Gulf. Florida closes flounder harvest October 15–November 30 and Alabama closes all of November, so the harvest window is September to mid-October.

Why do I miss flounder bites at Pensacola?

Flounder grab the bait crossways and turn it before swallowing. Feel the thump, drop the rod tip, count to three, then lean in — an instant hookset pulls the bait away.
