The morning number

How the bite score works.

The score combines three conditions that consistently shape an inshore Gulf Coast bite. It is a planning guide—not a promise from the fish.

  1. 40 Tide movement

    A larger predicted daily tide swing earns more points because moving water concentrates bait and usually improves feeding activity.

  2. 35 Wind

    Seven mph or less earns full points. The score drops as wind builds, with a four-point floor for this component.

  3. 25 Sky & rain

    Broken cloud cover scores best. Clear skies are moderate; rain chances and thunderstorms reduce the score.

Final score: Tide + wind + sky/rain, limited to 15–97. The best fishing window is calculated separately using sunrise and the closest morning tide event.
