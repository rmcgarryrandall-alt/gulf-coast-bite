Three waters, one regional report

Pensacola Bay

Fish the slower side of bridge current and the edges of pass water. At night, work the shadow line rather than the brightest water.

East Bay

Follow the cleanest shoreline and focus on grass points, creek mouths, and small drains as the tide begins moving.

Santa Rosa Sound

Start on shallow grass at first light, then shift toward channel edges and shaded docks as sun and boat traffic build.

What's biting through the year

Winter (Dec–Feb)

Sheepshead crowd the bridge rubble and Three Mile relics, black drum eat through the fronts, and trout stack in the deep bayous and rivers. Fish the warm afternoons.

Spring (Mar–May)

The sound and East Bay grass wake up: big trout at first light, redfish on the points, pompano along Pensacola Beach, and Spanish mackerel arriving under the birds in April.

Summer (Jun–Aug)

Dawn patrol season. Trout early over grass, mangrove snapper on every barnacled piling, white trout under the lights at night, and storms building most afternoons.

Fall (Sep–Nov)

The payoff: bull reds at Pensacola Pass, the flounder run through the drains, trout ganging up in the bays, and the mullet run pulling everything to the beach.

Common questions

What fish are biting in Pensacola right now?

The live dashboard at gulfcoastbite.com scores today's Pensacola Bay conditions from NOAA tide, wind, and sky data, and names a target species for the day. Seasonally: sheepshead and drum in winter, trout and pompano in spring, early-morning trout and snapper in summer, bull reds and flounder in fall.

Where can I fish from shore in Pensacola?

The bridge fishing piers on Pensacola Bay, the Bob Sikes bridge, the seawalls of the sound, and Pensacola Beach's surf all give shore anglers real water. Bring shrimp and something that gets to the bottom.

What's the best tide to fish Pensacola Bay?

Moving water beats any particular stage. The first hour after a tide change concentrates bait along the bridge lanes, pass seams, and grass points; slack tide is for re-rigging and coffee.
