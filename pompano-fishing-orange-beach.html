The local pattern

The Orange Beach sand between Perdido Pass and the state line holds run fish all spring. The beach re-cuts after every blow, so scout the troughs before committing; the pass-adjacent bars often dig the deepest lanes.

Pompano key on clean, green surf between 65 and 75 degrees. Read the beach first: the trough is the highway, the cuts are the on-ramps, and the moving tide is the schedule. A two-hook rig in the right water beats six rods in the wrong water.

Common questions

When do pompano run at Orange Beach?

March through May as surf temperatures pass 65°F, with a shorter second run in October. Sunrise on a moving tide with clean green water is the window worth protecting.

What rig catches pompano at Orange Beach?

A two-hook pompano rig with small circle hooks, bright floats, and a sinker that holds bottom, baited with sand fleas, fresh shrimp, or Fishbites, cast into the first trough.
