Privacy notice

Privacy, plainly.

Effective July 13, 2026 · Last updated July 24, 2026

Who operates this site

Gulf Coast Bite is owned and operated by R. McGarry CO. Questions about this notice may be sent to r.mcgarryrandall@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The catch journal stores species, fishing spot, region, length, note, and entry date in your browser’s local storage. Those entries remain on your device and are not transmitted to Gulf Coast Bite unless you explicitly choose to share a catch to the public catch board (see “Shared catches” below). Clearing browser data or using the delete control removes them.

Shared catches (optional)

If you check “Share to the public catch board” when logging a catch, the details you submit—species, length, the water or area you choose to show, your display name, note, photo, and that day’s conditions—are transmitted to Gulf Coast Bite, stored with its hosting provider (Netlify), and displayed publicly on this site. Only catches you explicitly opt in to share are transmitted. Choose your location setting deliberately and do not include personal or sensitive information in shared names, notes, or photos. The board keeps a limited number of recent entries, and photos are deleted when an entry is removed. To request removal of a shared catch, email r.mcgarryrandall@gmail.com.

Optional analytics

If you select “Allow analytics,” Google Analytics uses first-party cookies and receives information about site interactions, browser and device characteristics, and approximate geography. Gulf Coast Bite disables Google advertising personalization and Google Signals in its site configuration. You may choose “Essential only” or reopen “Privacy choices” in the site footer. Google’s handling of analytics information is governed by its own policies.

Gulf Coast Bite does not sell personal information and does not currently use analytics information for targeted advertising.

Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted by Netlify. Like most hosting providers, Netlify may process request details such as IP address, browser information, requested page, timestamp, and security diagnostics to deliver and protect the site. These records are handled under Netlify’s policies and retention practices.

Information you choose to send

If you contact an advertising or privacy email address, the message and contact details you provide are processed to answer your inquiry and retained only as reasonably needed for that purpose, business records, or legal obligations.

External services and links

The site retrieves public conditions from NOAA and the National Weather Service and links to Google Maps, tackle-shop websites, email applications, and other third-party destinations. Those services may collect information under their own privacy policies when you interact with them.

Location and sensitive information

Gulf Coast Bite does not request precise device location. The region buttons select general fishing areas and do not reveal where you are. Please avoid placing sensitive personal information in catch notes even though those notes are designed to remain local to your device.

Children

The site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. Gulf Coast Bite does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Choose “Essential only” to prevent the site from loading Google Analytics.
  • Delete catch-journal entries individually or clear the site’s local browser data.
  • Use browser cookie controls or the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Contact us with a privacy question or request concerning information you sent directly.

Changes to this notice

This notice may change as the site adds features, advertising, accounts, or data services. Material changes will be reflected by a revised date and, when appropriate, an additional notice on the site.
