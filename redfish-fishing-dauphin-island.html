The local pattern

Dauphin Island reds ride the current: bridge pilings on the seam, the West End cuts on a falling tide, and the grass flats behind the island for calm-day sight fishing. Sand Island's bar line holds bulls that the bridge crowd never meets.

Redfish are the year-round anchor here: slot fish on the flats, edges, and dock lines through the warm months, schooled in deep holes in winter, and oversize bulls staging near the passes each fall. Fish the moving tide and the current seams; the stage matters less than the movement.

Common questions

Where do bull redfish run near Dauphin Island?

During the September–November spawn run, oversize redfish stage around the nearest pass and bar system to Dauphin Island. Fish heavy jigs, big paddletails, or fresh cut bait on the moving tide, and release the big spawners quickly.

What's the best bait for redfish at Dauphin Island?

Live shrimp is the everyday answer, with gold spoons to cover water and cut mullet on bottom for bigger fish. Island Bait and Tackle on the island and Jemison's on the causeway have you covered.
