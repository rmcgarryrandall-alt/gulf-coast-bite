The local pattern

Orange Beach redfishing runs through Alabama Point: fish the color change on the incoming and the rock line seams. When traffic builds or wind ruins the pass, the Ono Island canals hold reds against shaded docks all day, and Cotton Bayou's drains work on the falling tide.

Redfish are the year-round anchor here: slot fish on the flats, edges, and dock lines through the warm months, schooled in deep holes in winter, and oversize bulls staging near the passes each fall. Fish the moving tide and the current seams; the stage matters less than the movement.

Common questions

Where do bull redfish run near Orange Beach?

During the September–November spawn run, oversize redfish stage around the nearest pass and bar system to Orange Beach. Fish heavy jigs, big paddletails, or fresh cut bait on the moving tide, and release the big spawners quickly.

What's the best bait for redfish at Orange Beach?

Live shrimp is the everyday answer, with gold spoons to cover water and cut mullet on bottom for bigger fish. J&M Tackle and the Caribe Marina dock store are the local bait stops.
