The local pattern

The Dauphin Island bridge is a sheepshead fortress from February into April: fish straight down against the pilings with fiddlers and strike at anything suspicious. The rubble at Katrina Cut holds a quieter second crowd.

Sheepshead own late winter: the spawn gangs the biggest fish of the year on jetties, bridge legs, and markers, and they bite right through the fronts that ruin everything else. Small strong hooks, tight line, and set on the tap you barely feel.

Common questions

When do sheepshead spawn near Dauphin Island?

February into April, when the biggest fish of the year gather on the local jetties, bridges, and markers around Dauphin Island. They bite through winter cold fronts that shut down trout and redfish.

What's the best sheepshead bait at Dauphin Island?

Fiddler crabs on a small, strong hook fished tight to structure. Live shrimp and barnacle or oyster pieces work too; Island Bait and Tackle on the island and Jemison's on the causeway have you covered.
