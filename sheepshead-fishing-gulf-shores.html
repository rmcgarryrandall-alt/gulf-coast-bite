The local pattern

The Gulf State Park Pier's pilings are the walk-up sheepshead spot in Gulf Shores, with the deeper Little Lagoon docks holding fish through winter. February through April is the show.

Sheepshead own late winter: the spawn gangs the biggest fish of the year on jetties, bridge legs, and markers, and they bite right through the fronts that ruin everything else. Small strong hooks, tight line, and set on the tap you barely feel.

Common questions

When do sheepshead spawn near Gulf Shores?

February into April, when the biggest fish of the year gather on the local jetties, bridges, and markers around Gulf Shores. They bite through winter cold fronts that shut down trout and redfish.

What's the best sheepshead bait at Gulf Shores?

Fiddler crabs on a small, strong hook fished tight to structure. Live shrimp and barnacle or oyster pieces work too; Hooked Up Bait & Tackle and Beach Bum Outdoors keep live bait through the season.
