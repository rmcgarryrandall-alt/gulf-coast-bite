The local pattern

The Alabama Point rocks and the Perdido Pass bridge legs hold the Orange Beach spawn crowd. Clear winter water lets you sight-fish the rocks on calm days; bring more fiddlers than pride.

Sheepshead own late winter: the spawn gangs the biggest fish of the year on jetties, bridge legs, and markers, and they bite right through the fronts that ruin everything else. Small strong hooks, tight line, and set on the tap you barely feel.

Common questions

When do sheepshead spawn near Orange Beach?

February into April, when the biggest fish of the year gather on the local jetties, bridges, and markers around Orange Beach. They bite through winter cold fronts that shut down trout and redfish.

What's the best sheepshead bait at Orange Beach?

Fiddler crabs on a small, strong hook fished tight to structure. Live shrimp and barnacle or oyster pieces work too; J&M Tackle and the Caribe Marina dock store are the local bait stops.
