The local pattern

Island trout stage off the bridge shadow lines at night and roam the grass behind the island by day. Spring wakes the flats behind the west end, and fall stacks fish in the deeper bay channels ahead of winter.

Trout are the timing fish: first two hours of light, first hour of moving tide. Spring puts the big females shallow at dawn, summer splits the day into a dawn bite and a night bite around lights, and fall regroups fish over deeper grass and shell.

Common questions

When is the best speckled trout fishing at Dauphin Island?

Late April through June for the biggest fish in shallow water, October–November for numbers, and the first two hours of daylight almost any warm month. Check the live Dauphin Island bite score before you commit a morning.

Are trout closed in February near Dauphin Island?

Florida's western Panhandle closes trout harvest during February (catch-and-release still allowed); Alabama stays open year-round. Verify current rules before keeping fish.
