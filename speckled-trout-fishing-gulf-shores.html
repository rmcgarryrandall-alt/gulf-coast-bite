The local pattern

Little Lagoon is the trout centerpiece in Gulf Shores: the pass seams on moving water, the west-end flats early in spring, and the deeper holes in winter. Bon Secour's grass and river mouth are the wind-day fallback that usually out-fishes the plan A.

Trout are the timing fish: first two hours of light, first hour of moving tide. Spring puts the big females shallow at dawn, summer splits the day into a dawn bite and a night bite around lights, and fall regroups fish over deeper grass and shell.

Common questions

When is the best speckled trout fishing at Gulf Shores?

Late April through June for the biggest fish in shallow water, October–November for numbers, and the first two hours of daylight almost any warm month. Check the live Gulf Shores bite score before you commit a morning.

Are trout closed in February near Gulf Shores?

Florida's western Panhandle closes trout harvest during February (catch-and-release still allowed); Alabama stays open year-round. Verify current rules before keeping fish.
