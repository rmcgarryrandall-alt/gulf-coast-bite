The local pattern

Mobile Bay may be the best trout water on this coast: causeway rips, gas rigs at daylight, and bars on both shores. In October and November, working birds over deep shell mark the schools; ease in from up-current and a jig does the rest.

Trout are the timing fish: first two hours of light, first hour of moving tide. Spring puts the big females shallow at dawn, summer splits the day into a dawn bite and a night bite around lights, and fall regroups fish over deeper grass and shell.

Common questions

When is the best speckled trout fishing at Mobile Bay?

Late April through June for the biggest fish in shallow water, October–November for numbers, and the first two hours of daylight almost any warm month. Check the live Mobile Bay bite score before you commit a morning.

Are trout closed in February near Mobile Bay?

Florida's western Panhandle closes trout harvest during February (catch-and-release still allowed); Alabama stays open year-round. Verify current rules before keeping fish.
