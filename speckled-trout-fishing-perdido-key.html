The local pattern

On the Key, the Old River grass and dock lights carry the trout year: topwater over the grass at gray light in spring, shrimp under the lights on summer nights, and fish staging off the pass edges when the water cools. The sound-side flats fish long after the Gulf side gets crowded.

Trout are the timing fish: first two hours of light, first hour of moving tide. Spring puts the big females shallow at dawn, summer splits the day into a dawn bite and a night bite around lights, and fall regroups fish over deeper grass and shell.

Common questions

When is the best speckled trout fishing at Perdido Key?

Late April through June for the biggest fish in shallow water, October–November for numbers, and the first two hours of daylight almost any warm month. Check the live Perdido Key bite score before you commit a morning.

Are trout closed in February near Perdido Key?

Florida's western Panhandle closes trout harvest during February (catch-and-release still allowed); Alabama stays open year-round. Verify current rules before keeping fish.
